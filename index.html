Project Scope

We are building a system that sends a current through the skin and extracts the response at several frequencies. The result is an impedance spectrum and it gives information about the structure of the tissue underneath.

Biopsies are invasive. Melanoma caught early has a five-year survival rate above 99%, and the one commercial EIS system is expensive and hard to access.
